# Hatcho iPad — exact image-generation prompts

Mode: built-in image generation, reference-based landscape recomposition.

## Butter Yellow

```text
Use case: compositing
Asset type: ONE horizontal 4:3 iPad wallpaper, cozy pixel art background.
Input role: the provided portrait butter-yellow wallpaper is the STYLE, EXACT PALETTE, AND PATTERN reference. Recompose it into LANDSCAPE 4:3; do not stretch the existing image or rotate the room.
Same butter yellow wall, honey-cream floor, very subtle faceless egg-shaped wallpaper motifs, roll-cake spirals, and tiny plus-shaped decorative marks. Keep motifs round and tiny, with the same square-pixel scale and low contrast. Extend the repeating pattern naturally across the wider wall.
Top 30% nearly empty plain flat wall area. Simple absolutely straight horizontal floor/baseboard line at 72% of the image height. Lower 28% simple horizontal plank floor with sparse seams. Flat frontal view, no perspective convergence, minimal calm cute warm room.
Native design intent 480x360 pixels, maximum 24 flat colors, no dithering, 4x nearest-neighbor export to 1920x1440. Crisp uniform square pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no painterly or photographic texture.
No text, characters, animals, UI, furniture, windows, plants, lamps, room corners, stage, pedestal, decorative props, or watermark. Faceless egg shapes are tiny wallpaper symbols, not actual eggs or living things. Preserve the reference's exact simple background style; no new scene details.
```
