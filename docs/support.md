## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account to use Hatcho?
No. You can use every core feature without signing in. Your focus history and collection are saved locally on your device.

### What does signing in do?
Signing in with Apple or Google backs up your focus history and collection to our server, so your progress survives reinstalls and syncs across your devices.

### How does hatching work?
Start a focus session. As you stay focused, your egg gradually cracks. When a session completes, the egg hatches into a creature that joins your collection. Rarer creatures appear less often.

### What is the charging effect?
When your device is plugged in and charging during an active focus session, your egg occasionally gets a small "zap" that nudges hatching progress forward. The bonus only applies while you are actively focusing.

### My creature isn't evolving. Why?
Creatures evolve by completing full "continue focusing" sessions after hatching. Each completed session advances one evolution stage, up to the final stage.

### Will I lose my creatures if I close the app?
No. Your active companion and your collection are saved and restored when you reopen the app.

### How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes all local data. If you signed in and want your backed-up account data deleted, email us at tlsrudgk13@gmail.com and we will remove it.

### Notifications aren't working.
Make sure notifications are enabled for Hatcho in iOS Settings → Notifications → Hatcho. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
