Privacy Policy

App: Hatcho  ·  Effective: June 30, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 30, 2026

Hatcho ("the App", "we", "us") is a focus-timer and creature-collection app. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. We designed Hatcho to work with as little personal data as possible.

Summary

  • You can use Hatcho without signing in. In that case, your data stays on your device only.
  • If you choose to sign in (with Sign in with Apple), your focus history and collection are backed up to our server so they survive across devices and reinstalls.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or tracking.

Information We Collect

1. Data you create in the App

  • Focus sessions — start/end times, planned and completed durations, and the timer mode you used.
  • Collection data — which creatures have hatched and when.

If you are not signed in, this data is stored only in the App's local database on your device (and in your device's own backup, e.g. iCloud device backup, which we do not control or access).

2. Account information (only if you sign in)

When you sign in with Sign in with Apple, we receive a unique account identifier and, depending on your choices with Apple, an email address. We use this solely to create and secure your account and to associate your backed-up data with you. We do not receive your password.

3. Device permissions

  • Notifications — if you allow them, we schedule local notifications on your device (for example, when a focus session finishes). These are generated on-device; we do not send push notifications from a server.
  • Battery / charging state — the App reads whether your device is charging to power an in-app gameplay effect. This is read on-device in real time and is not collected, stored, or transmitted.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or advertising identifiers.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the core timer and collection features.
  • To back up and synchronize your focus history and collection across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To keep your account secure.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

How Your Data Is Stored and Shared

When you are signed in, your data is stored on our backend infrastructure provided by Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL platform), which processes and stores data on our behalf. Access is protected by row-level security so that you can only read and write your own records.

We authenticate accounts through Sign in with Apple. Apple's handling of your sign-in is governed by their own privacy policy.

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties except as required to operate the App, comply with the law, or protect our rights.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data can be removed at any time by deleting the App from your device.
  • Account data: you can permanently delete your account and all backed-up data from within the App (account section → Delete account). You may also contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

Children's Privacy

Hatcho is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

International Users

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country. By using the App while signed in, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
